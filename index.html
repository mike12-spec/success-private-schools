Who we are

A school built around the child, not the timetable.

At Success Private Schools, learning is never one-size-fits-all. Our classrooms are shaped around how children actually grow — curious, active, and ready to be trusted with real responsibility. We pair academic rigour with the values and confidence that carry a child well beyond the school gate.

Located at Imam Babatunde Street in the heart of Ipaja, we welcome parents across Mosan, Pipeline Road and the wider community into a school designed for total development: mind, character, and community.
