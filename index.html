Our School

A school built around the child, not the timetable.

Success Private Schools was established in September 2005 to provide quality education in a safe and supportive environment for children in our community. Today we are a full Nursery, Primary and Secondary school serving families in Ipaja, with a second location in Aparadija, Ogun State.

Our Programmes

Our School Sections

Success Private Schools is made up of three sections: Nursery, Primary and Secondary.

01

Nursery

EARLY YEARS Our Nursery programme provides a safe and friendly environment where children develop early reading, writing, counting and social skills through classroom activities and play.
02

Primary

FOUNDATIONAL YEARS Our Primary programme builds on the foundation from Nursery, helping pupils develop their reading, writing, mathematics and other classroom skills, while encouraging good behaviour, confidence and participation in school activities.
03

Secondary

SENIOR YEARS Our Secondary programme covers Junior Secondary (JSS 1 to JSS 3) and Senior Secondary (SSS 1 to SSS 3), with Science, Commercial and Art options at the senior level, preparing students for their secondary school examinations.
