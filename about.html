About Us

Learn more about Success Private Schools, our story and our locations.

A school built around the child, not the timetable.

Success Private Schools is a Nursery, Primary and Secondary school serving families in Ipaja. Our classrooms are organised around each stage of a child's schooling, with clear routines for learning and behaviour.

Located at Imam Babatunde Street in the heart of Ipaja, we also have a second school in Aparadija, Ogun State. We welcome parents across both communities into a school that looks after both a child's learning and their wellbeing, every day.

Our Story

Success Private Schools was established on September 14, 2005, with the aim of providing quality education in a safe and supportive environment for children in our community.

Mission & Values

  1. 1 To provide academic excellence.
  2. 2 To inculcate moral standards for a total child.
  3. 3 To equip students for higher education.
  4. 4 To prepare students to be good and useful citizens.

Leadership

We aim to provide quality education to every child and provide them with an enabling environment which they can grow to reach their potential.
